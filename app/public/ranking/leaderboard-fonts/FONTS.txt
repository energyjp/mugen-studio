THE SCORE RANKING BOARD'S ARCADE FONTS
======================================

These 16 fonts are free, fan-made arcade fonts. Their makers released them
for anyone to use, without their names and without any license terms. They
are included here as they were released, so a host can pick them for the
Score Ranking leaderboard page. Mugen Studio's MIT license does not cover
them.

A Website export copies only the fonts a board picks, with this file, into a
leaderboard-fonts folder beside leaderboard.html.

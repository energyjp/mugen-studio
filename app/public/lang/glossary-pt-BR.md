# Glossary: Portuguese (Brazil), pt-BR

The term choices behind `pt-BR.json`. Use the same word for the same thing
everywhere; when you add or change a string, check it against this list first.

Tone: Brazilian Portuguese for a casual app. The reader is "você" everywhere
(never "tu"), with standard você imperatives ("solte", "clique", "veja",
"confira"). Buttons use the infinitive ("Adicionar", "Exportar", "Salvar");
tooltips that describe what a control does use the third person ("Abre...",
"Remove...", "Devolve..."). The band's tips keep their voices: WB in whole, dry
sentences; DJ AR all lower case; SHiZUKA lower case with kaomoji and "ne~".

Never use the European Portuguese words: ficheiro, ecrã, utilizador, telemóvel,
transferir (for download), aceder, registo, rato.

now speak pt-BR, so a quote of them uses their pt-BR words (the table below).

**Verb phrases joined into a sentence.** The Moves window builds "frames
before it {end}" and "it {end} after {n} frames" from short phrases ("ends",
"lands", "destroys itself"). In pt-BR those phrases are infinitives
("terminar", "aterrissar", "se destruir") and the sentences around them take
an infinitive: "antes de ele {end}", "vai {end} depois de {n} quadros".

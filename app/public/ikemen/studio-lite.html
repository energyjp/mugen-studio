Add characters, stages, and music, then press Build & Play. Everything is saved right here in your browser — no account, no upload.

⚠ ONLINE PLAY — READ THIS FIRST
To play online, you and your opponent must be running the exact same game: the Build ID on the loading screen must match, which means identical characters, stages and theme — anything added or removed here changes your build only. Easiest way to match: one of you presses Send my build, the other presses Import a build — then both loading screens show the same Build ID. Pick the same netcode on both sides. Connection quality depends on both players' networks — results may vary.
